Long reading

Read the long reading. Then play the audio and listen to the pronunciation. Say it with the person speaking.

My family is new to Rockville! We met lots of people at the community picnic. First, we met Max and Jack. They are brothers. Max is a firefighter and Jack is a police officer. Next, we met two sisters. They work at the hospital. Yumi is a doctor and Kimi is a nurse. Lastly, we met a teacher, a librarian, and a bus driver from my new school. I forget their names! My parents work in our new community too. Dad is a mail carrier and Mom is a crossing guard. Rockville rocks!
