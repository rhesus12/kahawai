Ali's House short stories

Read each short story. Then play the audio and listen to the pronunciation. Say it with the person speaking.

Reading 1

Ali’s house is grey. It has a black roof and a black door. There is a garage and a lot of windows. There is a flower box in one window.

Reading 2

Ali has a front yard and a backyard. There is a deck with two chairs and a table. There is a white fence around the yard. In the backyard, there is a garden and a swing set.

Story and questions

This is Ali’s house. It is grey.

The front door is black. The roof is black too.

Ali’s house has a garage.

It also has a lot of windows.

Ali has a flower box in the front window. There are red flowers in the flower box.

Ali’s front yard has a deck.

There are two chairs and a table on the deck. There is also a white fence around the front yard.

In Ali’s backyard, there is a swing set and a garden.

There is a white fence around the backyard too.

Questions

1. What colour is Ali’s house?
2. What colour is Ali’s door?
3. Does Ali’s house have a garage?
4. What colour are the flowers in the flower box?
5. What is on the deck?
6. Where is Ali’s garden?
