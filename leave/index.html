Asking for Leave

Read the dialogue. Then listen to the audio. Read along with the audio and practise saying the dialogue.

Dialogue 1

Audio:
Worker: Hi John, I would like to take leave on Thursday.

Boss: Of course! When exactly, and is everything okay?

Worker: Yes, all good — I just have a doctor's appointment in the afternoon.

Boss: No problem at all. Take leave and feel free to head out after lunch. Hope it goes smoothly!

Worker: Thank you so much, I really appreciate it!

Dialogue 2

Audio:
Manager: Hi, can I help you?

Worker: Yes. I would like to take some leave, please.

Manager: Why do you need leave?

Worker: I am going to a wedding in Auckland.

Manager: When is the wedding?

Worker: It is next Friday.

Manager: How many days do you need?

Worker: I need three days, Friday, Saturday and Monday.

Manager: OK. When will you come back to work?

Worker: I will come back on Tuesday.

Manager: That's fine. Please put in a leave request.

Worker: Thank you. I will do that today.

Manager: You're welcome. Have a good time at the wedding.

Worker: Thanks!
