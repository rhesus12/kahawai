Two Listening Stories

Five Years in New Zealand

Listening audio

Language focus: Present simple · Past simple · have / has · can / can’t · present simple negatives · frequency expressions · future plans

Five years ago, I came to New Zealand from Tonga to study. I was a student at a college in Wellington. I lived near my college and made some good friends. We studied together and helped each other.

Now I have a good group of friends. Every day, I get up at seven o’clock. I have breakfast and walk to college. I study in the morning and have lunch with my friends. In the afternoon, I go to the library or work on my assignments. In the evening, I cook dinner and sometimes watch a film with my friends.

I can speak English well now, but I still work hard to improve. I can understand my teachers and can talk to people easily. I can’t always understand fast English.

I miss my family in Tonga. I have a mother, father, two brothers and a sister. I call them every weekend. My family is proud of me, and they always ask about my studies.

Now I am close to finishing my course. I have only a few assignments left. I feel excited about the future, but I also feel sad because I will miss my friends in New Zealand. I am going to visit Tonga in two months, and I am really looking forward to seeing my family again.

My First Job in London

Listening audio

Language focus: Present simple · Past simple · have / has · can / can’t · present simple negatives · frequency expressions

Two years ago, I left New Zealand and moved to London for my first job. I was excited, but I was also nervous. I had never lived overseas before. I found a small flat with two other young women.

Now I have a full-time job in an office in central London. Every morning, I get up at seven o’clock and have breakfast. I walk to the train station and take the train to work. I usually arrive at eight thirty. I work with a friendly team, and we often have lunch together.

At first, I couldn’t understand everyone because they spoke very quickly. Now I can understand my colleagues much better. I can also use the computer systems at work, but I can’t do everything yet.

After work, I sometimes meet my friends. We go to a café or watch a film. At the weekend, I often explore London. I like seeing new places, but I miss New Zealand and my family.

I call my parents every Sunday. They have always supported me. I am happy with my new life, and I hope to stay in London for another year.
